1. Institutional Architecture & Role Authorities

Security hierarchy, dynamic permission inheritance, and portal URL prefixes.

UPLYFT employs a high-security Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) system with multi-tenant isolation. Every user is authenticated, associated with an active campus/institute, and bound to a specific URL prefix and granular permission set.

Principal / Executive

Prefix: /principal
Unrestricted master authority across all campuses, academic terms, timetable generator, payroll, and staff rights delegation.

Delegated Admin / Coordinator

Prefix: /staff
Master delegated staff empowered with granular administrative rights (admissions, timetable, staff, invoices, directory, security).

Faculty / Teacher

Prefix: /teacher
Classroom operations: assigned timetable, attendance rosters, daily diary postings, LMS materials, mock exam generation, and grading.

Accountant / Bursar

Prefix: /accountant
Financial hub: fee vouchers, bank challan printing, payment reconciliation, general ledger, AI expense calculation, and salary sheets.

Student & Guardian

Prefix: /student
Personal learning portal: fee challan downloads, schedule viewer, learning materials, 24/7 AI tutor, practice quizzes, and report cards.

Front Office / Admin Staff

Prefix: /admin
Front desk operations: student admissions registration, staff onboarding, visitor logs, and preliminary fee voucher collections.

Step-by-Step: How to Generate an Institutional Timetable

1

Define Campus Days & Hours

Click Days & Hours. Select teaching days (e.g. Mon–Fri), specify campus opening time, and define lunch / break intervals.

2

Assign Teachers to Subjects

Navigate to Subject-Teacher Allocations. Assign qualified faculty to each class section and configure weekly period quotas.

3

Execute AI Generation Engine

Click Auto-Generate AI Timetable. The system calculates clash-free room, teacher, and section allocations in seconds.

4

Review Grid & Publish

Inspect the visual grid for highlighted conflicts. Use drag-and-drop or manual adjusters, then click Export Excel or broadcast to student portals.

How to Delegate Coordinator Rights (Step-by-Step)

1

Select Staff Member

2

Click the Toggle Delegation switch to enable Master Delegated Administrator privileges.

Check specific checkboxes for modules the coordinator will manage (e.g. Student Registration, Timetables, Fee Invoices, Directory).

Coordinator can now sign into /staff/dashboard and will see active management cards matching their assigned rights.
